    One book, two covers.

    The app and the landing page share one brand: Midnight navy at night, paper by day, ink outlines, violet for what you touch and a hanko seal for who we are. The app keeps each platform's own controls and text face. The brand lives in colour, outline, metadata and the moments around reading.

    Shaddai Readerシャダイ リーダー샤다이 리더沙代阅读

    Three panels ink in manga order, right first: 読 for Japanese, 읽 for Korean, 读 for Chinese, each in its own lettering. As each panel lands, the name appears in that language: シャダイ リーダー, 샤다이 리더, 沙代阅读. Then the gutters close, the panels become one hanko seal, it presses 読 and "Shaddai Reader" lands. In the app it plays once on the splash screen, in 2.4 seconds. Everywhere else the logo is the pressed seal.

    Violet is the accent: buttons, selection, focus and progress. Hanko red is seal ink. It marks the brand (the logo, stamps, the noren, chapter numbers) and is never on a control, so nobody confuses it with an error. Danger is crimson, always with an icon and a label. Source colours stay fixed because they name a service, not the app.

    OutlinesThe ink line is the structure.

    1px hairline

    Rows, dividers and app cards. Ink at 16% inside lists, full ink around cards.

    2px control

    Buttons, inputs, pills, covers and the tab bar's top edge.

    Manga panels on the landing page; the Home hero and empty states in the app.

    Outlines are ink: white on Midnight, black on Washi. No soft drop shadows anywhere. The only offset shadow is the violet one under a primary action. iOS glass blur stays where the platform expects it, edged in 2px ink.

    Do and don't

    Do

    • Give each screen one violet action. Keep hanko red for seals and marks.
    • Keep metadata in the brand mono.
    • Outline in ink, separate with space.
    • Leave the reader screen bare. The pages are the content.

    Don't

    • Put real covers from the library on public pages.
    • Use CJK display faces for body text or controls.
    • Show danger without an icon and a label.
    • Add soft shadows or gradients to chrome.
